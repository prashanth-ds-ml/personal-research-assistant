# Incident Response Runbook

## Severity levels
- **SEV1**: customer-facing outage. Page the on-call engineer immediately; open a bridge call within 10 minutes.
- **SEV2**: degraded service. Respond within 30 minutes during business hours.

## Postmortems
Every SEV1 requires a blameless postmortem within 5 business days, reviewed by the engineering director.
