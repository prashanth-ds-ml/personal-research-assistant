## Pretraining Objectives and Reasoning Capabilities

The impact of pretraining objectives and initialization on reasoning and transliteration accuracy varies significantly across architectures. [inference] Recent findings suggest that while reinforcement learning explicitly induces reasoning, base models may already possess inherent reasoning capabilities due to extensive chain-of-thought data during pretraining [E4]. However, deductive reasoning remains unreliable in pretrained language models (PLMs), which often fail to generalize learned logic rules and suffer from catastrophic forgetting when fine-tuned for reasoning tasks [E5]. Empirical analysis indicates that finetuning improves skills like textual entailment but risks overfitting to prompt templates rather than acquiring robust reasoning abilities [E6].

Regarding transliteration, models trained with Masked Next Token Prediction (MNTP) objectives on multilingual corpora demonstrate consistent improvements over original multilingual pretrained language models for languages written in phonetic scripts [E11]. Specifically, TRANSMI-modified models outperform baselines across Cyrl, Arab, Deva, and Other script groups, whereas gains are minimal for logographic (Hani) or Latin (Latn) scripts due to inherent ambiguity or lack of diacritic changes [E11]. In contrast, specific fine-tuned models like `pavanmantha/qwen3-4b-telugu-transliteration` utilize cross-entropy loss computed only on masked assistant responses to achieve high accuracy in Telugu-to-Roman conversion [E12].

Performance metrics for tool calling and reasoning capabilities differ by model size and initialization. [inference] Qwen3-4B-Instruct achieves an AUROC of 0.948, outperforming Llama-3.1-8B-Inst (0.927) but trailing larger Qwen3 variants like the 14B and 32B models [E9]. [cited; subject not in the cited evidence] While IndicBERT-v3-4B utilizes a rigid curriculum learning approach to balance English and Indic proficiency, its bidirectional architecture differs from causal LLMs used for standard next-token prediction objectives [E13].
